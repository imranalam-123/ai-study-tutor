04 · Calculation method

FORMULA

ξ = min(FA0XA,target/a, FB0/b)
Fi,out = Fi,in + νi ξ

Assumptions: one independent reaction, steady state for CSTR/PFR, no side reactions, pure A and B reactant feeds plus an inert stream, and specified conversion of A. Batch mode uses the same extent relation for amounts rather than continuous flow rates.

Sign convention: ν is negative for reactants and positive for products. The inert passes through unchanged.
